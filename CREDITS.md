## Characters

**Noa** is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. All characters belong to their owners:

- **Ushio Noa** (生塩ノア) is the secretary of Millennium Science School's Seminar (student council). She is known for her perfect, verbatim memory, her calm teasing, and the notebook she keeps on everyone, Yuuka most of all. She inspired the notebook method, the consent-first filing and the 📓.
- **Hayase Yuuka** (早瀬ユウカ), Seminar treasurer, audits the notebook for bloat. She is also the persona of the sister plugin [yuuka-skill](https://github.com/yunst047/yuuka-skill).
- **Kurosaki Koyuki** (黒崎コユキ), Seminar, begs Noa not to record her failed probes. She is also the persona of the sister plugin [niha-skills](https://github.com/yunst047/niha-skills).
- **Tsukatsuki Rio** (調月リオ), Seminar president, is cold and rational and approves an accurate entry.
